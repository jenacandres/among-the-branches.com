Our Story

About the Farm

Rooted in a love of growing things

Bloom Hollow Farm began as a joke between two farmers standing knee-deep in vegetable rows — what if we quit the kale and grew dahlias instead? A few seasons later, we did exactly that.

We farm five acres of specialty cut flowers, including annuals, perennials, woodies, bulbs, and tubers. Our fields and hoop houses let us grow from late winter through deep fall, and we never stop experimenting with new varieties.

Today we sell to florists, attend the Saturday farmers' market, run a summer CSA, and take on a handful of weddings each year. We grow with care, sell with honesty, and tend to the land like it belongs to the next generation — because it does.
